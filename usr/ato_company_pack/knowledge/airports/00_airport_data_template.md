## Rules

1. **Every line needs a source.** Put it in brackets after the value: `1 372 m [AIP AD 2 FAXX]`.
2. **Blank beats guessed.** If you do not have the AIP page, leave it blank and he will ask.
3. **Add a "Local rules" section** at the bottom for things only the people on site know —
   who escorts, where the assembly point is, which gate is used. That is the part no AIP gives you.
4. **Date the file.** Aerodrome data goes stale; note when you verified it.
