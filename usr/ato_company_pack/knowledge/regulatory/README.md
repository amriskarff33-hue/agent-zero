# Regulatory

Drop here: the standards you work to — SACAA CARs and SA-CATS, ICAO Annexes and Docs,
NFPA standards, ACI and IATA material, OHS Act, NRTA.

Store the **reference** and cite it. Do not paste licensed text (NFPA, ACI, IATA) into knowledge
you will regenerate courses from.

Examples: `car-part-139.pdf`, `sa-cats-139.pdf`, `icao-annex-14-vol1.pdf`, `nfpa-1003.pdf`.
