# Training Centre Alliance

Drop here: material that comes from the alliance rather than from AM Risk alone — shared course
material, common standards, assessment rules, brand and logo rules, member obligations, agreed
terminology.

Keep it in this one folder, separate from your own documents. It is not yours alone, so:

- Anything you would not want a client to see stays out of client-facing output.
- If the arrangement changes, this folder can be excluded or removed without touching your own
  knowledge.
- Where alliance rules and AM Risk rules differ, say so in a short note in this folder —
  otherwise he will guess which one applies.

Examples: `alliance-quality-manual.pdf`, `alliance-shared-avop-material.docx`,
`alliance-brand-rules.pdf`, `alliance-member-obligations.pdf`.
