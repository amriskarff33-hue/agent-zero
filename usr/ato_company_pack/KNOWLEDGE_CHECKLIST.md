# What to collect

Work down this list. Each item makes him measurably better at a different job.

Run `python3 tools/pack_manage.py checklist` to see which ones you already have.

## Start here — the highest value

| # | Document | What it teaches him |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One complete course you are proud of (all documents) | Your structure, depth and standard |
| 2 | The exam paper and memorandum for it | How you assess and at what standard |
| 3 | Your lesson plans / facilitator notes | How you sequence and pace |
| 4 | Company profile or capability statement | Who he works for |
| 5 | One course you consider weak | What to avoid — as useful as a good example |

## Then fill these in

| Area | Documents |
|---|---|
| **Company** | ATO certificate, service catalogue, organogram, key contacts, brand guide |
| **Clients** | Client and airport list, appointment letters, contracts, SLAs |
| **Operations** | Training delivery SOP, assessment and moderation policy, record-keeping, POPIA procedure |
| **Regulatory** | The CARs / SA-CATS parts you work to, ICAO Annexes, NFPA 1003 / 403, ACI, IATA AHM |
| **Airports** | One aerodrome data pack per airport you train at |
| **Alliance** | Shared course material, common standards, brand rules, member obligations |
| **Media** | Logo, brand masters, photo library index |

## Rules

- **Start small.** Ten well-chosen documents beat two hundred random ones.
- **Quality over quantity.** One excellent course teaches him more than ten mediocre ones.
- **Personal data:** learner names, ID numbers and results are POPIA-regulated. Keep them on
  local models, never on a cloud provider.
- **Licensed material:** reference it, don't paste it.
